The three problems

parser: the shared parser reads every date month first. Every tenant on file writes the day first, so a day-first date comes out with day and month swapped. This comes in by app, by email, and by booking-v2 forms, whose hint already asks for the day first.

hint: booking-v3’s hint asks for the month first. The form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field, the tenant’s people type what it asks, and the form reads it day first. Only form reports from booking-v3 tenants. Fixing the parser does not fix this one; the form has to print the hint it reads.

one-day: dates move by one day. A swap of day and month cannot turn 12 May into 11 May. These reports say the same meeting shows a different day in Lisbon and in Toronto, and an invite arrives before its booking: a time zone problem. The desk already noted them as one-day moves; the #4471 page still files them under the parser.

Why one ticket hid them

  • Merging went by subject line (decision 0001). All three problems arrive as “wrong date”, so they all merged into #4471.
  • Intake’s tag is a guess from the reporter’s browser: it tags hint whenever the browser is en-US. The cause comes from the tenant and the way in, so the tags disagree with it (see intake tagged above).
  • The #4471 page’s own table showed hint as “not this ticket — no ticket of its own”, under a callout that says one issue.

For the desk

  • Before you merge into a big ticket, check that the report shows the same symptom: a swap, or a one-day move.
  • Count by report id, never by export row.
